# DISCIPLE_JOURNEY.md

# The Disciple Journey

## Purpose

The Lively Stones platform exists to guide people from **curiosity about Christ** to **mature discipleship and spiritual leadership**.

Every feature should move a disciple forward in this journey.

The goal is not simply user engagement—it is spiritual transformation.

---

# Journey Overview

```text
Visitor
    ↓
Interested Guest
    ↓
Registered Disciple
    ↓
Onboarding
    ↓
Daily Spiritual Habits
    ↓
Community Engagement
    ↓
Learning & Discipleship
    ↓
Serving
    ↓
Leadership Development
    ↓
Mentoring Others
```

Every user should always know:

* Where they are.
* What they should do next.
* How they are growing.

---

# Stage 1 — Visitor

## Goal

Introduce the ministry and invite people into a relationship with Christ and the community.

### User Needs

* Learn about LSIN.
* Read teachings.
* View events.
* Explore resources.
* Join the community.

### Platform Focus

* Ministry website
* Events
* Sermons
* Testimonials
* Contact
* Registration

### Success

Visitor creates an account.

---

# Stage 2 — Registration

Registration should feel like joining a family—not filling out paperwork.

Collect only essential information:

* Name
* Email
* Password
* Phone (optional)
* Invite code (optional)

After registration:

Never redirect directly to the dashboard.

Instead:

→ Start onboarding.

---

# Stage 3 — Onboarding

## Purpose

Help the disciple personalize their journey.

### Collect

* Profile photo
* Bio
* Location
* Church/Fellowship
* Occupation/School
* Spiritual interests
* Areas for growth
* Prayer interests

### Introduce

* Mentor
* Community
* Bible Reading
* Prayer
* Learning
* Events

### Success

Profile reaches 100% completion.

Only then should the disciple enter the main application.

---

# Stage 4 — Daily Spiritual Habits

This is the heart of the platform.

Every login should encourage daily fellowship with God.

## Daily Home

Instead of a dashboard full of cards:

Present a daily journey.

Example:

Good morning.

Today's Verse

Continue Reading

Prayer Focus

Today's Devotional

Upcoming Meeting

Community Highlights

Continue Course

Prayer Requests

Journal Reminder

Everything should encourage one next step.

---

# Stage 5 — Scripture Engagement

The platform should encourage consistent Bible reading.

Features include:

* Reading plans
* Continue reading
* Daily verse
* Highlights
* Notes
* Bookmarks
* Reading history
* Memory verses

The objective is consistency rather than speed.

---

# Stage 6 — Prayer Life

Prayer should become a daily habit.

Support:

* Prayer journal
* Prayer requests
* Prayer reminders
* Prayer partners
* Group prayer
* Answered prayers

The platform should celebrate faithfulness rather than perfection.

---

# Stage 7 — Learning

Every disciple should grow in biblical knowledge.

Learning includes:

* Courses
* Sermons
* Bible studies
* Assignments
* Quizzes
* Reflection questions
* Reading materials

Learning should be structured into progressive pathways.

---

# Stage 8 — Community

Christian growth happens in relationship.

Encourage:

* Discussions
* Testimonies
* Encouragement
* Questions
* Accountability
* Prayer support
* Small groups

Community should remain respectful, encouraging, and Christ-centered.

---

# Stage 9 — Serving

Disciples should discover opportunities to serve.

Possible areas:

* Media
* Prayer
* Bible Study
* Evangelism
* Follow-up
* Administration
* Hospitality
* Technical Team

The platform should connect gifts with opportunities.

---

# Stage 10 — Leadership Development

As disciples mature, they should be equipped to lead.

Leadership tools may include:

* Training tracks
* Mentor feedback
* Leadership resources
* Team management
* Ministry responsibilities

Leadership is viewed as service, not status.

---

# Stage 11 — Multiplication

The highest stage is not personal achievement.

It is helping others grow.

Mature disciples become mentors.

Mentors invest in new disciples.

Healthy disciples multiply.

---

# Notifications

Notifications should encourage spiritual growth rather than demand attention.

Examples:

* Continue today's Bible reading.
* Remember today's prayer focus.
* Your mentor shared an encouragement.
* Bible study starts in one hour.
* Someone prayed for your request.

Avoid unnecessary notifications.

Every notification should have spiritual value.

---

# Weekly Rhythm

Every disciple should naturally experience:

Daily

* Bible reading
* Prayer
* Reflection

Weekly

* Community discussion
* Bible study
* Worship
* Mentor interaction

Monthly

* Goal review
* Spiritual reflection
* Ministry participation

---

# Success Indicators

A disciple is progressing when they:

* Read Scripture consistently.
* Pray regularly.
* Participate in community.
* Complete learning pathways.
* Attend gatherings.
* Serve faithfully.
* Encourage others.
* Grow in Christlike character.

---

# Product Principle

The platform should never ask:

> "How can we keep users online longer?"

Instead ask:

> **"How can we help this disciple take one meaningful step closer to Christ today?"**
