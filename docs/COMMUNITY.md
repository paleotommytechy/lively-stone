# COMMUNITY.md

# Community System

## Purpose

The Community System exists to cultivate authentic Christian fellowship, encouragement, accountability, and mentorship.

It is **not** a social media platform.

The objective is to help disciples grow together in Christ.

Every interaction should build faith, strengthen relationships, and encourage spiritual maturity.

---

# Community Philosophy

Community should promote:

* Love
* Encouragement
* Accountability
* Wisdom
* Prayer
* Service
* Discipleship

It should never reward:

* Popularity
* Comparison
* Arguments
* Self-promotion
* Endless scrolling

The platform should encourage meaningful participation rather than passive consumption.

---

# Community Principles

Every community feature should answer one question:

> **Does this help believers encourage one another and grow together in Christ?**

If not, it should not be built.

---

# Community Structure

```text
Community

├── Events
```

Each section has a clear purpose.

---

# Community Feed

The feed should prioritize value over recency.

Recommended order:

1. Mentor posts
2. Ministry announcements
3. Prayer requests
4. Testimonies
5. Bible discussions
6. Encouragement posts
7. Event reminders

Avoid algorithmic feeds designed to maximize attention.

---

# Post Types

Supported content:

## Encouragement

Short messages that strengthen believers.

---

## Testimony

Stories of God's faithfulness.

---

## Prayer Request

Requests for individual or group prayer.

---

## Bible Reflection

Personal insights from Scripture.

---

## Discussion

Questions or conversations around biblical topics.

---

## Announcement

Official ministry communication.

Only authorized roles may publish announcements.

---

# Community Reactions

Avoid traditional social media reactions.

Preferred reactions:

* 🙏 Praying
* ❤️ Encouraged
* 📖 Inspired
* 🙌 Amen
* 🤝 Standing With You

Avoid:

* Downvotes
* Angry reactions
* Competitive popularity metrics

Reaction counts should encourage support rather than comparison.

---

# Comments

Comments should:

* Encourage
* Clarify
* Teach
* Pray
* Support

Discourage:

* Arguments
* Personal attacks
* Political debates
* Gossip
* Unverified doctrine

---

# Prayer Requests

Prayer requests are a first-class feature.

Each request supports:

* Title
* Description
* Privacy Level
* Category
* Status
* Answered Testimony

Privacy options:

* Private
* Mentor Only
* Small Group
* Community

Answered prayers should remain linked to the original request.

---

# Bible Discussions

Dedicated area for discussing Scripture.

Examples:

* Daily reading reflections
* Bible study questions
* Difficult passages
* Memory verses
* Application discussions

Bible discussions should remain searchable.

---

# Testimonies

Purpose:

Celebrate God's faithfulness.

Structure:

* Story
* Scripture
* Date
* Category
* Photos (Optional)

Testimonies should remain permanently accessible.

---

# Mentorship

Mentorship is different from general community.

* Mentor messages
* Progress reviews
* Prayer support
* Resource recommendations
* Follow-up reminders

Mentor conversations remain private unless explicitly shared.

# Search

Users should search:

* Posts
* Testimonies
* Bible discussions
* Prayer requests
* People
* Events

Search should prioritize relevance over popularity.

---

# Moderation

Every community must remain Christ-centered.

Moderators may:

* Hide content
* Remove content
* Warn users
* Suspend posting
* Escalate reports

Moderation decisions must be logged.

---

# Reporting

Users may report content for:

* Abuse
* Harassment
* False teaching
* Spam
* Inappropriate content

Reports should include optional comments.

---

# Community Guidelines

Members should:

* Speak truth in love.
* Encourage one another.
* Respect different levels of spiritual maturity.
* Protect confidentiality.
* Build unity.
* Avoid unnecessary controversy.

The platform should visibly reinforce these values.

---

# Notifications

Community notifications should include:

* Someone prayed for you.
* Mentor commented.
* New testimony.
* Bible discussion reply.
* Prayer request update.
* Event invitation.

Avoid unnecessary notification noise.

---

# Privacy

Users control the visibility of:

* Prayer requests
* Testimonies
* Profile details
* Activity status

Private content must never appear in public feeds.

---

# Accessibility

Community should support:

* Images with alt text
* Keyboard navigation
* Screen readers
* Responsive layouts
* Clear typography

Everyone should be able to participate.

# Success Metrics

Healthy community is measured by:

* Prayer participation
* Testimony sharing
* Constructive discussions
* Mentor engagement
* Community retention
* Event participation
* Encouragement given
* Spiritual growth indicators

Not by:

* Likes
* Followers
* Time spent scrolling
* Viral content

---

# Engineering Principles

Always:

* Paginate feeds.
* Sanitize user-generated content.
* Rate-limit posting.
* Audit moderation actions.
* Enforce RLS.
* Cache expensive queries.
* Optimize media delivery.

Never:

* Build infinite scrolling without purpose.
* Expose private posts.
* Trust client-side moderation.
* Encourage addictive engagement patterns.

---

# Product Principle

> **Community exists to help believers walk together toward Christ. Every interaction should encourage faith, strengthen relationships, and inspire spiritual growth—not compete for attention.**
