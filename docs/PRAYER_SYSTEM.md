# Prayer System

## Purpose

The Prayer System exists to help believers cultivate a consistent, intimate, and transformative prayer life.

Prayer is not a feature of the platform—it is one of the primary ways disciples commune with God.

The platform should encourage users to pray **daily, intentionally, and together**.

---

# Design Philosophy

Prayer should feel:

* Personal
* Peaceful
* Sacred
* Encouraging
* Hope-filled
* Distraction-free

The interface should create space for reflection rather than urgency.

---

# Core Principle

> **The platform should never replace prayer. It should simply help believers pray more faithfully.**

---

# Daily Prayer Journey

Every day should naturally guide the disciple through prayer.

```text
Today's Prayer Focus
        │
        ▼
Spend Time With God
        │
        ▼
Prayer Journal
        │
        ▼
Pray For Others
        │
        ▼
Reflect
```

Prayer is a journey, not a checklist.

---

# Prayer Home

The Prayer page should contain:

* Today's Prayer Focus
* Prayer Journal
* Personal Prayer Requests
* Community Prayer Requests
* Prayer Partners
* Answered Prayers
* Prayer History

The first thing users see should inspire prayer—not statistics.

---

# Prayer Journal

Every disciple has a private prayer journal.

Features:

* Rich text
* Voice notes (Future)
* Images (Optional)
* Scripture references
* Tags
* Mood
* Date

Journal entries remain private by default.

---

# Prayer Requests

Users can create prayer requests.

Fields:

* Title
* Description
* Category
* Urgency
* Privacy
* Answer Status

Categories:

* Personal
* Family
* Ministry
* School
* Work
* Health
* Finances
* Missions
* Thanksgiving

---

# Privacy Levels

Every prayer request supports:

* Private
* Mentor Only
* Prayer Team
* Small Group
* Community

Users remain in control of their privacy.

---

# Prayer Partners

Users may invite trusted believers as prayer partners.

Prayer partners can:

* Encourage
* Pray
* Follow updates
* Celebrate answered prayers

Prayer partnerships are mutual and optional.

---

# Prayer Circles (Future)

Support small prayer groups.

Examples:

* School of Tyrannus
* Leadership Team
* Campus Fellowship
* Families
* Departments

Each circle includes:

* Shared requests
* Prayer schedule
* Prayer wall
* Group journal
* Meeting reminders

---

# Prayer Reminders

Users may schedule reminders.

Examples:

* Morning Prayer
* Midday Prayer
* Evening Prayer

Reminders should remain gentle and encouraging.

Avoid overwhelming notifications.

---

# Answered Prayers

Every request can become a testimony.

Support:

* Date answered
* Story
* Scripture
* Reflection

Answered prayers encourage faith across the community.

---

# Prayer Calendar

Users can review:

* Daily prayers
* Weekly prayers
* Monthly reflections
* Special fasting periods

This provides perspective on God's faithfulness over time.

---

# Prayer & Scripture Integration

Every prayer request may include:

* Related Bible verses
* Suggested Psalms
* Prayer prompts
* Daily devotional links

The Bible and Prayer systems should complement one another.

---

# Mentor Integration

Mentors may:

* Pray with disciples
* Recommend Scriptures
* Send encouragement
* Follow up on requests (when permitted)

Private requests remain protected according to user privacy settings.

---

# Community Prayer

The community may:

* Pray for requests
* Leave encouragement
* Share testimonies
* Celebrate answered prayers

Avoid public debates or unsolicited advice.

Prayer comes before discussion.

---

# Prayer Streaks

Track consistency—not competition.

Display:

* Days prayed
* Weekly rhythm
* Monthly consistency

Never display public leaderboards.

Prayer is a personal discipline.

---

# Prayer Insights

Provide gentle insights such as:

* You've been consistent this week.
* You've completed your morning prayer three days in a row.
* Remember to revisit older prayer requests.
* Celebrate this answered prayer.

Insights should encourage, never shame.

---

# Accessibility

Support:

* Voice input (Future)
* Large text
* Screen readers
* Dark mode
* Offline drafting

Prayer should remain accessible in every environment.

---

# Notifications

Examples:

* Today's prayer focus is ready.
* Your mentor prayed for you.
* Someone is praying for your request.
* A prayer reminder is due.
* Celebrate an answered prayer.

Notifications should inspire prayer rather than demand attention.

---

# Future Features

Potential additions:

* Prayer timer
* Guided prayer sessions
* Fasting plans
* Family prayer spaces
* AI-assisted prayer prompts (optional and clearly distinguished from Scripture)
* Audio prayer journal
* Church-wide prayer campaigns

---

# Success Metrics

Measure:

* Prayer consistency
* Prayer journal usage
* Answered prayers recorded
* Community prayer participation
* Mentor prayer engagement

Do not measure:

* Longest prayer
* Public rankings
* Number of requests alone

The objective is faithfulness, not performance.

---

# Engineering Principles

Always:

* Encrypt sensitive data where appropriate.
* Respect user privacy.
* Cache non-sensitive metadata.
* Audit moderator actions.
* Enforce Row Level Security.

Never:

* Expose private prayer requests.
* Use prayer data for advertising or profiling.
* Encourage comparison between believers.
* Treat prayer as a productivity metric.

---

# Product Principle

> **Prayer is a conversation with God, not a task to complete. The platform should gently invite believers into consistent communion with Him while protecting the privacy, dignity, and sacredness of every prayer.**
