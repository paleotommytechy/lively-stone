# Product Strategy

**Product Name:** Lively Stones International Network Platform

**Product Category:** Digital Discipleship Platform

**Current Version:** v2 (Redesign)

**Status:** Product Definition

---

# Product Vision

Lively Stones is a digital platform that helps believers consistently grow in Christ while giving ministry leaders the tools to disciple, mentor, organize, and shepherd people effectively.

Rather than being another ministry website, the platform functions as a **Digital Discipleship Operating System**—bringing together Scripture, prayer, community, learning, mentorship, and ministry administration into one unified experience.

---

# Problem Statement

Many believers desire spiritual growth but struggle with consistency due to fragmented tools and limited accountability. Ministry leaders also face challenges in tracking discipleship progress, organizing resources, managing events, and maintaining meaningful engagement with members.

Existing solutions often focus on only one aspect of Christian life (such as Bible reading, messaging, or event management) instead of supporting the complete discipleship journey.

---

# Product Mission

To leverage technology in helping believers:

* Know Christ deeply.
* Grow consistently in spiritual disciplines.
* Build authentic Christian community.
* Serve faithfully.
* Become disciples who make disciples.

At the same time, provide ministry leaders with practical tools to shepherd people with wisdom, organization, and accountability.

# Design Principles

Every screen should feel:

* Peaceful
* Focused
* Intentional
* Encouraging
* Simple
* Beautiful
* Fast

# Out of Scope (v2)

The following are intentionally excluded from the initial redesign:

* Live streaming
* Financial accounting
* Church management (full ERP)
* Online giving
* AI chatbot
* Marketplace
* Video conferencing

These may be considered after establishing a strong discipleship foundation.

---

# Product Principle

> **The goal is not to build the biggest Christian platform. The goal is to build the platform that most consistently helps people become more like Christ.**
